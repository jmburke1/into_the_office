# Into The Office

A full-fledged TUI game about exorcizing a demon-infested haunted office building!

## The Legend of the Blackwood Building

In 1987, the Blackwood Corporate Center was a symbol of modern ambition (in spite of its small stature), a gleaming, yet small and simple office building in the heart of suburbia. It had its hands in everything from cutting-edge tech startups to venerable law firms. But beneath the polished marble floors and behind the glass walls, a dark secret festered.

During construction, the building's foundations were laid over an old burial ground that the developers had dismissed as mere folklore. The ground was disturbed, the restless dead awakened, and something ancient slumbering in the earth was unleashed.

On Halloween night of 1987, the building went dark. Every employee, every janitor, every visitor vanished without a trace. The only thing left behind were their desks, their phones, and their loved ones nightmares about where they had gone! The building was condemned, its windows boarded, its name erased from the city's memory.

But the building never truly emptied.

Twenty years later, a private equity firm bought the property cheap, eager to transform it into a trendy tech hub. They stripped the old carpeting, installed sleek desks, and opened the doors. But the lights still flicker. The elevators still go to floors that don't exist. And the shadows still move.

You are a paranormal investigator with a unique skill set: you can see the unseen, hear the unheard, and banish the uninvited. Your mission: enter the Blackwood Corporate Center and exorcize the demonic forces that have taken residence.

But be warned - the building doesn't want you there. The demons don't want to leave. And the deeper you go, the more you'll discover that the evil within isn't just haunting the building - it's alive, it's hungry, and it's growing stronger with every day you delay.

The clock is ticking. The office is calling. Are you brave enough to face what lurks behind the cubicles?
